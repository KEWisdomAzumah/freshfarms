# freshfarms
FreshFarms is a digital farm management and an AgriFood-network platform.

Fresh Farm is a data-driven technology built to aid farmers, increase yield, and maximize sales profit. It is designed to connect farmers, wholesalers, households, consumers, and data in one convenient location to promote direct sourcing, reduce overpricing of products, and promote best farm practices by farming with data.
Fresh Farm aims at providing an affordable and healthier alternative to traditional markets by reducing the role of middlemen, thereby allowing consumers to have access to fresh and high-quality food at competitive prices, while enabling farmers to receive fair value for their produce and an advantage to farm with data.
